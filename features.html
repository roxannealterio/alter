Exploreour features

Your program, picked for you

Answer eighteen questions and it picks one of twelve programs for you, and tells you why that one. Change it any time in two taps.

ALTER YOU tells you to go heavier

Hit every rep last week and it tells you to add weight, and exactly how much. Fall short and it holds you there until you are ready.

See how every lift should feel

A video on every exercise, and cues for what you should be feeling and where. If something is off, it tells you how to fix it.

Calories made for you, not a flat 2,000

Worked out from your height, weight, age and how much you actually move. Scan a barcode or log it in grams.

Training that moves with you

It tracks your period, ovulation and PMS, then adjusts your training and your calories to match where you are.

Progress you can actually see

Measurements, photos, the weight on the bar and the sessions you have done. The scale is in there, it is just not the headline.

101 recipes, ready to go

Every one is built around your macros, with the calories and protein already counted. Cook it, tap it, it is logged.

Your shopping list, already done

Pick your recipes for the week and it builds one list, sorted by aisle. Tick things off as you shop and it remembers.
