What's inside

Everything you get

A program built 100% around you

A few questions, then we build your training and your nutrition from the answers.

  • Your goal, your days and how long you have.
  • Where you train, and what equipment you have.
  • Your experience, your injuries and what you eat.
  • Every choice explained as you make it.
  • It rebuilds every week from your check in.

Your week, already planned

Your next session is on the home screen. Open it and start.

  • Two to six sessions a week, set from your answers, with rest days in.
  • Follow along sessions when you only have ten minutes.
  • Warm ups and the Form Library on the same screen.
  • Travel mode swaps the week to no equipment.
470 cal

Science-based nutrition

Calories and macros set from your body and your goal. Every recipe has its calories and macros set per serving.

  • A deficit on a fat loss plan, a surplus when you are building.
  • Switch programs and your targets move with you.
  • 100+ recipes, ready to go, built around your macros.
  • Filter by calories, macros, dietary needs and meal type.
  • Cooking for more than one? Adjust servings with the Serving Size tool.

The Form Library

How-to videos for every movement, on the same screen as your session.

  • Search any movement by name.
  • Form cues on every exercise.
  • Tempo, RPE and rest on every set.
  • Hit every rep and the app adds weight to your next session.
  • Miss a rep and the weight stays the same next session.

Cycle syncing mode

Your cycle, pregnancy and postpartum all change what your body needs.

  • Your period, ovulation and PMS are predicted from your own dates.
  • Your training and your calories move with them.
  • Conception mode for your fertile window and ovulation day.
  • Pill mode for when predictions do not apply. An estimate, not contraception.

Progress you can see

Measurements, photos, the weight on the bar and the sessions you have done.

  • Waist, hips and thighs, week by week.
  • Photos side by side, taken weeks apart.
  • Volume and consistency, every week.
  • Badges at a tonne lifted, and at thirty days in.

Mindset

Nineteen short reads, grouped into journeys.

  • Journeys for starting out, food, training and headspace.
  • A read picked for the phase of your cycle you are in.
  • One thing to try that day, at the bottom of each read.
  • Mark each one read and pick up where you left off.

My Diary

A record of each day, and the goals you are working on.

  • A to do list that rolls what you miss over to tomorrow.
  • Set a goal and give it a weekly target.
  • A gratitude line and a daily mood check.

The community feed

Finish a session and post it to the feed, with a photo if you want.

  • Other women on the same programs, posting the same week.
  • Like and comment on each other’s sessions.
  • Add friends and your feed is just them.

Your grocery list made easy

Pick your recipes for the week and your shopping list builds itself.

  • Sorted by aisle, so you are in and out of the shop.
  • Tick things off as you shop and they stay ticked.
  • Change a recipe and the list changes with it.
