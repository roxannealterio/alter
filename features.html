A program built 100% around you

Answer questions about your goal, your days, where you train and what you eat. Your training and your nutrition are both built from the answers.

  • Where you train, and what you have to train with.
  • Your experience, your injuries and what you eat.
  • Every choice explained as you make it.

Science-based nutrition

Personalised calories and macros for your body and your goals, not a flat 2,000 for everyone.

  • A real deficit on a fat loss plan, a surplus when you are building.
  • Switch programs and your targets move with you.
  • 101 recipes, ready to go, built around your macros.
  • The calories and protein are already counted.
  • All you do is make it and press log.

See how every lift should feel

When something feels wrong, the cue tells you what to change.

  • A video on every exercise.
  • Cues for what you should be feeling.
  • Tempo, RPE and rest on every set.
  • Hit every rep and the weight goes up, by an exact amount.
  • Fall short and it stays where it is until you are ready.

Training and nutrition that moves with you

Your cycle, pregnancy and postpartum all change what your body needs.

  • Your period, ovulation and PMS are predicted from your own dates.
  • Your training and your calories move with them.
  • Conception mode for your fertile window and ovulation day.
  • Pill mode for when predictions do not apply.

Progress you can see

Measurements, photos, the weight on the bar and the sessions you have done.

  • Waist, hips and thighs, week by week.
  • Photos side by side, taken weeks apart.
  • Volume and consistency, every week.
  • Badges for real things, like a tonne lifted.
  • The scale is one line, never the headline.
