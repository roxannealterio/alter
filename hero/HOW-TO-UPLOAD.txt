HERO PHOTOS
===========

Two training photos for the home page hero. One fills the screen at a
time and they fade between each other every two seconds.

  hero_1.jpg  Push up on dumbbells   (shows first)
  hero_2.jpg  Weighted hip thrust

NOTHING TO UPLOAD
-----------------
They sit in the site's own "hero" folder and show the moment the site
goes up. Uploading a hero photo in the admin still overrides the file
here, so nothing is lost either way.

To change one, replace that file on the server with another photo of the
same name. To swap which shows first, swap the two files.

THE SPARES
----------
hero_3.jpg (bodyweight squat) and hero_4.jpg (lying leg raise) are still
in this folder but are not used by the page. Kept so either can go back
in without a reshoot. Delete them if you are sure.

A NOTE ON SHAPE
---------------
Each photo fills the whole hero, which is a wide letterbox on a laptop
and a tall portrait on a phone. The same file has to work as both, so
only the middle of the frame is guaranteed to survive. Keep the subject
central and leave room around them.

Both have a warm grade applied to skin tones only, so the grey backdrop
stays neutral. hero_1 has had its floor extended at the bottom so the
subject sits higher in frame.
