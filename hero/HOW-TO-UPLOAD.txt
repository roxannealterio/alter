HERO PHOTO
==========

One photo fills the top of the home page.

  hero_1.jpg  Roxy, seated, black bodysuit

There used to be a second one fading in and out every two seconds.
That was removed on 24 September. One still photo, no movement.

NOTHING TO UPLOAD
-----------------
It sits in the site's own "hero" folder and shows the moment the site
goes up.

TO CHANGE IT
------------
Replace hero_1.jpg on the server with another photo of the same name.

Make it a tall portrait shape, around 1029 x 1809. Anything wider gets
cropped in from the sides, so keep the important part of the picture
away from the left and right edges.

IF THE OLD PHOTO KEEPS COMING BACK
----------------------------------
A photo uploaded in the admin overrides the file here. If you replace
hero_1.jpg on the server and still see the old one, the database has a
copy. Go to the admin, find the home hero slot, and upload the new
photo there too.
