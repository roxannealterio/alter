Training style and experience

Heavy compound work for your whole body, four days a week, with three day and two day versions as well. Gym or home, matched to the equipment you have. Every lift has a tempo, a target effort and a how-to video in the Form Library, so a first timer knows what to do and someone experienced still gets pushed.

Workout duration

About 45 minutes a session, with a guided warm up before it. If you are away, travel mode drops the equipment from your week and puts it back when you are home.

Nutrition

Your own calorie and protein targets, set from your body and from what you are trying to build, with a floor they cannot drop below. 100+ recipes with the macros set per serving, a barcode scanner and a grocery list that builds itself.

Flexible dieting, so nothing is off the menu. There is a Big Mac in the food library.

What it will not do
  • Change you in a fortnight. It runs for twelve weeks.
  • Hand you a list of foods you are not allowed.
  • Replace real support if you are struggling with food or your body right now. Please read this first.

Frequently asked questions

When does it start and can I join late?

It starts Monday 2 November, and sign ups close Sunday 1 November at 11:59pm. Everyone starts on the same day and finishes Sunday 24 January 2027, so sign ups do not reopen once it has begun.

I have never lifted weights. Is it for me?

Yes. Every exercise has a how-to video in the Form Library and cues for what it should feel like, and the weights start at whatever you can lift today.

What happens to my photos?

Progress photos are private to you and stored securely. You only share them if you enter them for the $500, and nothing is ever posted publicly without you saying so.

What if I am on my period or my cycle is all over the place?

Both your sessions and your targets shift with where you are in your cycle, worked out from the dates you log. Cycle syncing mode adapts your training and your food to each phase of your month, so a heavy week is planned for. More on how that works.
