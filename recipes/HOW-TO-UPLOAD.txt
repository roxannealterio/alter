RECIPE PHOTOS
=============

Five food photos, resized for the website.

NOTHING TO UPLOAD
-----------------
These sit in the site's own "recipes" folder and show the moment the
site goes up. No admin step, the same way the transformation photos
work.

To change one later, replace that file on the server with another photo
of the same name.

  recipe_1.jpg  Strawberry overnight oats
  recipe_4.jpg  Chipotle chicken tacos
  recipe_5.jpg  Proper chicken burgers

The first three match the recipes that were already on the homepage. The
tacos and the burgers are new cards, so they need the macros checked
against the real recipes in the app before launch. I estimated them:

  Tacos    510 cal, 44g protein, 52g carbs, 14g fat
  Burgers  620 cal, 48g protein, 58g carbs, 20g fat

If the app says something different, the app wins. Tell me the real
numbers and I will correct the website.

A NOTE ON SHAPE
---------------
The burger photo is landscape and the rest are portrait. The recipe cards
crop to a square, so the burger photo loses its left and right edges. If
you have a portrait version of that shot it will sit better.
