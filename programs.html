Questions about the programs

Are these programs suitable for beginners?

Yes. Foundations is four weeks of learning the lifts, three full body sessions a week, six movements, with a video demonstration on every single one. Most women who quit training quit because they started with something too hard, too soon. Every other program also scales to your experience, and the app asks about that before it picks one for you.

Can I follow them at home, without a gym?

Yes. Dumbbell Strength needs only dumbbells, and Bodyweight Only needs no equipment at all. Every other program has a home version too. The app asks where you train and what you have to train with before it builds anything, so you are never handed a session you cannot do.

How many days a week do I need?

Between two and six, and you choose. Busy Girl Reset is built for the weeks when time is short, and two good sessions you actually do beats four you never start. Your plan is built from the number of days you tell it you have, rather than quietly handing you a five day one.

Do I get all twelve programs, or just one?

All twelve are included in one membership, and you can switch between them in two taps whenever you want a change of pace. Nothing is sold separately, there is no upgrade tier, and the challenges are included as well.

What if none of the twelve suit me?

Then the app builds one that is one hundred per cent yours, from your goal, your days, your equipment and your experience. It is not a template with your name on it, and it rebuilds every week from your check in as you get stronger.

Is there anything for pregnancy or postpartum?

Postpartum Rebuild starts with core and pelvic floor work before it adds any load back. Your training and your calories also shift through pregnancy and postpartum rather than being treated as ordinary training weeks.
