Why they work

Motivation fades.A deadline doesn't.

Left to our own devices, we all wait for "one day". A challenge turns that into day one. You get a clear plan, a set window, and the pull of a whole community showing up beside you, so you stay in it long enough for the work to actually show.

How it runs

Four steps,every round

  1. 01

    Register in the app

    Tap join before the start date and lock your spot. Everyone begins together on day one.

  2. 02

    Get your plan

    Training and macros built around your answers. You’re never left guessing what to do.

  3. 03

    Train with the group

    Log every session, chase your numbers, and lean on a whole community pushing through the same weeks.

  4. 04

    Finish and compare

    Look at the data: the sessions you did and the weight you added, next to where you started.

Every round has a winner. I judge the transformation across the whole block: your before and after photos alongside the training you logged, the strength you added and the sessions you turned up for. It is judged, never drawn.
