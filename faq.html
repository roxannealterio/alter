FAQ

Commonquestions

Is this another quick fix?

No, and I won’t pretend otherwise to sell you a subscription. Nothing in ALTER YOU works in four weeks. Pick a program, give it eight, and the numbers move. That’s the whole secret, and it’s why every program tells you how long it takes before you start it.

I’ve never lifted weights. Is it for me?

Foundations exists for exactly this. Three full body sessions a week, six movements, done properly. Most people who quit training quit because they started with something too hard, too soon. The form library will also tell you what each lift should feel like, and what it feels like when it’s wrong.

I only have two days a week.

Then you get Two Day Minimum, and it’s not a consolation prize. Two good sessions you actually do beats four you never start. Tell it you have two days and it builds you a real two day program, rather than quietly handing you a five day one.

Do I need a gym?

No. Home Strength runs on dumbbells and Bodyweight Base runs on nothing at all. It will never recommend a program that needs equipment you don’t have.

Are my progress photos private?

Yes. They are stored so that only your account can read them. Another signed in user can’t reach them by any means. They are never used for anything else, and when you delete your account the image files are deleted too, not just the database rows.

How does the cycle syncing work?

Tell it when your last period started and it adjusts your training and nutrition guidance across your cycle. Your energy in week one isn’t your energy in week three, and pretending otherwise is how women end up feeling broken for no reason. Cycle predictions are estimates and must never be used for contraception.

What is cycle syncing?

Matching how you train and eat to where you are in your menstrual cycle rather than doing the same thing every week. Most women can handle more load in the first half, when oestrogen is climbing, and feel flatter in the week or so before their period. Cycle syncing plans for that instead of treating a hard week as a failure of willpower.

Should I lift weights on my period?

Yes, if you feel up to it. Plenty of women train well during their period, and some feel stronger once it starts because the pre-period slump has lifted. If you are cramping badly or exhausted, ALTER YOU pulls the session back rather than telling you to push through. Nothing is lost by taking a lighter day.

Why am I weaker the week before my period?

In the luteal phase your core temperature is higher, your heart rate sits higher for the same effort, and fatigue comes on sooner. That is physiology, not laziness. The same weight genuinely feels heavier. ALTER YOU expects it, drops the target for that week, and picks the load back up afterwards.

Does it work if my cycle is irregular?

Yes. It uses your logged dates rather than assuming a textbook twenty eight days, and it gets more accurate as you log more. If your cycle is very irregular the phase guidance will be looser, so treat it as a prompt to check in with how you feel rather than a rule. Irregular cycles are worth mentioning to your doctor.

What does it predict?

Your next period, ovulation, your fertile window and the days PMS is likely to land, along with the phase you are in right now and what to expect from your energy and your lifts. It works off the dates you log rather than a textbook twenty eight days, so it sharpens every month. Every one of those is an estimate, not a certainty.

What is conception mode?

An optional setting for women who are trying to get pregnant. Turn it on and you see today's chance of conceiving, your fertile window and your ovulation day. It is off unless you switch it on, and nobody else sees it. It is an estimate based on your logged dates, not a fertility treatment and not a diagnosis, and it is not contraception either. Anything to do with fertility belongs with your doctor.

Can I see my symptoms over time?

Yes. Log how you feel each day, cramps, bloating, mood, energy and flow, and after a couple of cycles ALTER YOU shows you where they cluster, like low mood landing mostly in your luteal phase four times out of five. What matters is seeing that the week you think you are failing is the same week every month.

Can I use it if I am on the pill?

Yes, and plenty of women do. There is a birth control setting: turn it on and ALTER YOU stops showing you ovulation predictions that would not mean anything, and tracks what still does. Hormonal contraception flattens the natural hormone swing, so the phase to phase differences may be smaller or barely there. The training and the nutrition still work exactly the same. You can also turn cycle syncing off entirely in settings if it is not telling you anything useful.

Can I use it with PCOS or endometriosis?

The programs and the nutrition side work the same for anyone. The cycle predictions assume a reasonably regular cycle, so with PCOS in particular they may not line up with what your body is doing, and you can switch that part off. ALTER YOU is not medical advice and does not diagnose anything. Anything to do with managing a condition belongs with your doctor.

Where do my calorie targets come from?

Your height, weight, age, activity level and goal, using the Mifflin St Jeor equation. Not a flat 2,000 for everyone, which is what most apps quietly do. You can change any of it in Settings and the targets update immediately.

Can I log food in grams?

Yes, and grams is the default. Scan a barcode and it fills the grams in for you. Servings are there if you want them, but if you own kitchen scales you shouldn’t have to pretend you don’t.

How do the challenges work?

Each one runs for a set block, usually eight to twelve weeks, and every round is included with your subscription. The prize goes to the best transformation: your before and after photos, the strength you added, the sessions you completed and the habits you kept. I judge the transformation across the whole block, from your before and after photos alongside what you logged.

How do I cancel?

iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then ALTER YOU. Two taps and it’s done. I don’t hide the button or make you email me.

Is this medical advice?

No. ALTER YOU gives general fitness and wellbeing guidance. It’s not a medical device. Speak to a healthcare professional before starting a new programme, especially if you’re pregnant, postpartum, injured, or managing a health condition.
