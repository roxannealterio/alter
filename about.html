Meet Roxy and Chey

The facesbehind the app

Two is better than one.

Ten years plus in the fitness industry between us, and 500+ women coached in person.

We built ALTER YOU because we kept seeing the same thing: women doing everything they’d been told to do and getting nowhere with it. We knew we could do it better.

We believe you can do anything you put your mind to. We’re not just here to help you build the body of your dreams, we’re here for the mind that goes with it. Because it was never only how you look. It’s how you feel.

Roxy’sstory

Starting any journey can be tough, but everyone begins somewhere.

For me it started at 42kg, with an eating disorder, a negative mindset towards health and fitness, and a body I was destroying with endless cardio, overtraining and under-eating. That cycle made me feel insecure and unhappy every day. I found myself in tears one day, questioning whether any of those habits were even worth it.

Then I realised I could change, and that I could love myself, and that health and fitness could be something I actually enjoyed. So I took action, to reach my goals and become the best version of myself. The same way you can. The journey is hard, but those are the moments you grow and learn the most.

Two years on I loved my body, I was using food as fuel, and I was enjoying my training. It was the happiest I had ever felt.

Then I started competing in bodybuilding, and found myself in another extremely toxic relationship with my body. It took me somewhere I had already been once. What finally worked was balance: looking good and feeling my best at the same time, instead of trading one for the other. That is the whole thing, and it is what I coach now.

You don’t need a story like mine to be here. Most of the women I’ve coached have never had an eating disorder. They wanted to lose weight, or get strong, or carry their kid up the stairs without stopping. Everyone is welcome. Join the community today.

Looking back, I wonder how much quicker I would have got there if I’d had someone to push me and give me guidance. That’s why I started coaching, and it’s why I built ALTER YOU.

Never underestimate what you and your body can achieve. You will be incredibly proud of how far you have come. It is truly rewarding.

Chey’sstory

I’ve faced my own health struggles.

I battled eating disorders from the age of 14 through to my early twenties. After countless fad diets, I deeply empathise with women who feel trapped in the same cycle. In my teens I lived in fear of food, and it cost me both physically and mentally. Under-nourishing while overtraining left me with declining health, no energy, the loss of my cycle, body dysmorphia and constant anxiety.

At 21 I decided to change my life. By 22 I was in the fitness world and competing in bodybuilding, not to punish my body but to finally understand it. Through education, patience and proper nourishment I rebuilt my relationship with food, with movement, and with myself.

Becoming a mother changed that understanding again. Pregnancy, postpartum recovery and breastfeeding were the most challenging and humbling seasons of my life. I had to relearn how to coach my own body through constant change, surrender control, and respect a body that was no longer only mine. Watching it grow, sustain, birth, heal and nourish life gave me a deeper admiration for the female body than I thought possible. Strength took on a new meaning. Progress looked different. Self-compassion stopped being optional.

Now, with lived experience, education and an unwavering respect for the female body, I am passionate about helping women nourish, honour and trust their bodies through every stage of life.

This isn’t about shrinking yourself. It’s about building resilience, confidence, and a body that supports your life rather than controls it.

Why the app is like this

Everything in hereis years of coaching

Every decision in ALTER YOU comes from years of coaching real women in person, and seeing what works and what holds women back.

  1. 01

    The scale is never the only headline

    Your weight matters to you, and that is fair enough. But if you lift while you lose fat, the number on the scale is the least informative thing in the room, and treating it as the score has broken more women than it has helped.

  2. 02

    There’s a Big Mac in the food library

    No food is banned. Not one. A plan you have to break isn’t a plan, and the moment an app tells a woman a food is forbidden, it has started teaching her the thing I spent years unlearning.

  3. 03

    Your calories can’t be set dangerously low

    The app will not let your calories go too low. Not if you ask it to, and not if you want it to. This is the single decision I’m most certain about.

  4. 04

    It tells you to go heavier

    Most women train for a year and lift the same weight the whole time, because nothing ever tells them to add to the bar. ALTER YOU does, the week you have earned it, and it won’t flatter you in the weeks you haven’t.

  5. 05

    It will never panic you

    No streak about to die. No red badge. No notification at 9pm telling you that you have failed. You’re an adult with a life, and the app is a tool, not a supervisor.
