My story

I built ALTER YOU because I kept seeing women do everything they'd been told and get nowhere with it.

Everyone begins somewhere. I began a long way from here: a negative mindset towards health and fitness, and a body I was wearing down with endless cardio, overtraining and under-eating.

So I changed what I was doing. I ate more, I cut the cardio back and I started lifting. I learned how food and training work, and set targets I could hit week by week.

What worked in the end was balance: training hard, eating enough, and keeping both going at once. That's what I coach now.

 
Meet Chey, our coach

Chey coaches women through pregnancy, postpartum and breastfeeding. In her words:

“I've faced my own health struggles. In my teens I was afraid of food, and under-nourishing while overtraining left me with no energy and constant anxiety. At 21 I decided to change things. I studied, I ate enough, and I gave it time.

Becoming a mother changed it again. Pregnancy, postpartum recovery and breastfeeding were the hardest things I've done. Now I coach women through those stages, with food and training that fit what the body is doing at the time.

Eat enough, lift, rest, and give it months. That's what I tell every woman I coach.” Chey

 

You don't need a story like ours to be here. Most of the women we coach just want to get strong, feel good and see progress. Everyone is welcome.
