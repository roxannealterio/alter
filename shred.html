Is Summer Shred for you?

It is, if your goal over the next ten weeks is to:

  • Lose fat and still have the muscle at the end of it
  • Eat in a deficit that still leaves you able to train
  • Train to a plan instead of making it up each week
  • Start now rather than waiting for the new year
Training style and experience

Heavy compound work for your whole body, four days a week if you have them and a real three or two day version if you do not. Gym or home, matched to the equipment you have. Every lift has a tempo, a target effort and a form video, so a first timer knows what to do and someone experienced still gets pushed.

Workout duration

About 45 minutes a session, with a guided warm up before it. If you are away, travel mode drops the equipment from your week and puts it back when you are home.

Nutrition

Your own calorie and protein targets, set from your body rather than a template, with a floor they cannot drop below. 101 recipes with the macros already counted, a barcode scanner and a grocery list that builds itself.

Flexible dieting, so nothing is off the menu. There is a Big Mac in the food library.

What it will not do
  • Change you in a fortnight. It is ten weeks and it is meant to be.
  • Hand you a list of foods you are not allowed.
  • Replace real support if you are struggling with food or your body right now. Please read this first.

Frequently asked questions

When does it start and can I join late?

It starts Monday 12 October, and sign ups close the night before. Everyone starts the same day, which is most of the reason it works, so there is no joining in week three.

I have never lifted weights. Is it for me?

Yes. Every exercise has a video and cues for what it should feel like, and the weights start where you are rather than where someone else is.

What happens to my photos?

Progress photos are private to you and stored securely. You only share them if you enter them for the $500, and nothing is ever posted publicly without you saying so.

What if I am on my period or my cycle is all over the place?

Both your sessions and your targets shift with where you are in your cycle, worked out from the dates you log rather than a textbook 28 days. A heavy week is expected rather than treated as you failing. More on how that works.
