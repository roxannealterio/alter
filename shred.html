What it is

Twelve weeks,finished before Christmas

Twelve weeks from 28 September puts the finish line the week before Christmas, and that’s on purpose. You won’t be scrambling three weeks out from Christmas. You’ll have been at it since spring.

The food

A deficit you canactually live on

This isn’t a crash diet. You eat a bit less than usual, not so little that you’re miserable by week three. And the app won’t let you drop your calories too low, even if you try.

  • Targets from your height, weight, age and how much you move
  • Protein stays high, so you keep what you built
  • 101 recipes, logged in grams or scanned from a barcode
  • Nothing is banned. There is a Big Mac in the food library.

The training

Keep liftingheavy

Go light while you’re eating less and you just end up a smaller version of the same shape. Keep the weights heavy and your body holds onto the muscle while the fat comes off.

  • Four days if you have them, three or two if you do not
  • Tempo and target effort on every set
  • A form guide on every lift
  • Your cycle is built in, so a heavy week is expected rather than a failure

How it runs

Four steps,twelve weeks

  1. 01

    Save your spot now

    Doors close Sunday 27 September. Put your name down before then and your place is held, and everyone starts together on the Monday, which is most of the reason it works.

  2. 02

    Get your program and your numbers

    Training and calories built from your answers: your days, your equipment, your experience. You are never left guessing what to do.

  3. 03

    Twelve weeks with the group

    Log every session. Keep lifting heavy while the calories come down, so what you lose is fat rather than the muscle underneath it.

  4. 04

    Finish in December

    Look at the data: the sessions you did and the weight you held onto, next to where you started.

What the twelve weekswill and won’t do

What you get

  • Fat loss that keeps the shape underneath it
  • A deficit you can hold for twelve weeks without crashing
