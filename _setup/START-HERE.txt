ALTER YOU — everything for launch
=================================

FOUR STEPS
----------
1. UPLOAD
   Everything in this folder goes to your web root, keeping the folder
   structure. "results", "recipes" and "app" stay as folders.

2. DATABASE
   Supabase, SQL editor, paste in the whole of _setup/SETUP.sql and run
   it. Once. Safe to run again any time, it never overwrites your work.

3. CHECK
   Open alteryouapp.com/check.html and read it. It tests the stylesheet,
   the script, the fonts, the database and the photos, and names the fix
   for anything that failed. Delete check.html when you are happy.

4. THE APP STORE LINK
   Open alter.js, find line 1, paste your App Store URL:

       window.ALTER_APP_STORE = 'https://apps.apple.com/au/app/...';

   That points every download button and all three price cards at once.
   Until you set it they scroll to the signup form, so nothing is a dead
   link in the meantime.


THE SITE WORKS BEFORE YOU UPLOAD A SINGLE PHOTO
-----------------------------------------------
The app screenshots, the recipe photos and the transformations all ship
with the site and show immediately. Anything with no photo yet shows a
faint version of your A mark rather than a blank box.

Every one of them can still be changed in the admin under Website
Images. An upload there overrides the shipped file.

Sharing works too. og-image.jpg sits in the root, so posting your link
shows a picture from day one.


THE SUMMER SHRED CAMPAIGN
-------------------------
  Saturday 19 September   Pilates in the Park, the launch event
  Sunday 27 September     Doors close on registrations
  Monday 28 September     Twelve weeks start, everyone together
  Monday 21 December      Finish, the week before Christmas

The bar at the top of every page moves through those by itself: counts
down to the event, then to doors closing, then to the Monday, then hides
itself. The countdown on shred.html does the same. Nobody has to change
any wording on the day.

To move a date, edit these three attributes on the bar in every page:

    data-event="2026-09-19T09:00:00+10:00"
    data-close="2026-09-27T23:59:00+10:00"
    data-start="2026-09-28T05:00:00+10:00"

and the two on the countdown in shred.html.


YOUR LEAD LIST
--------------
Everyone who fills in a form lands in the admin under People, then
Leads. Search, filter, mark them contacted or joined, export a CSV.

The consent column decides what you may send. A tick means they agreed
to hear from you generally. A blank means the Spam Act only lets you
contact them about the thing they signed up for, so filter to
"consented only" before exporting for a general campaign.

Formspree still emails you a copy of every submission, so nothing is
lost if the database has a bad day.


WHO IS PAYING
-------------
The dashboard shows members, sessions, active users and leads straight
from the app. It cannot see who is subscribed, because App Store billing
runs through RevenueCat. _setup/REVENUECAT.md connects the two in about
ten minutes and adds Paying and On trial to the dashboard.
