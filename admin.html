Leads

Everyone who has filled in a form on the website. Yours to keep, and yours to export. The consent column is the one that matters before you send anything: a tick means they agreed to hear from you generally, and a blank means you may only contact them about the thing they signed up for.

Write once, then take it wherever you send from. Who it goes to is set by the filters on the Leads tab, so filter there first and the count here follows. Anyone who has unsubscribed is always left out, whatever the filters say.

Take it somewhere

A browser cannot send hundreds of emails or texts on its own, and a page that could would have to carry a sending key that anyone could read out of it. So this prepares the message and the list, and you send from a tool that does it properly. "Open in my mail app" is fine up to about twenty people; past that use the CSV in Mailchimp, Klaviyo or similar.

The designed versions of these templates, the ones with the wordmark and the sage buttons, are in the emails folder of your site files. Copy one into a mail tool that takes HTML.

If you would rather send straight from here, there is a ready made Supabase function in supabase/functions/send-broadcast in your site folder. Deploy it with your Resend key and this page can call it.

Announcements

Announcements appear in the app and members get a notification next time they open it. Good for "Bikini Build starts Monday", or anything else you want members to see.

Transformations

Client before and after photos for the homepage and glute page. Always get written permission before you put someone's photo up.

App home image

The photo behind the greeting on the app home screen. Upload one and it updates for everyone. A darker or sage-toned landscape image reads best behind the text. Leave it empty to use the plain gradient.

Website Images

Every photo spot on the website, grouped by the page it appears on. Upload one and it replaces the placeholder on the live site straight away, no republishing needed. The size note under each one is the shape it gets cropped to, so pick a photo that suits it.
If this list is empty, run SITE_IMAGES.sql in Supabase once.

Blog

Articles you publish here appear on the website blog straight away. Add a cover image and it shows on the card and at the top of the article.

Exercises and videos

Every exercise in the app, with its set up, movement, mistakes and cues already written. Click one to upload the video members watch when they tap "how to".

Recipe Photos

Click a photo to upload or replace it. It updates in the app straight away.

Progress Photos

These are private client photos. They load through secure, expiring links and are only visible to you here. Filter by a challenge to see just that round's submissions.
